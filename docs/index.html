How do I arrange a pickup?

Use the booking form and we'll prepare a WhatsApp message with your request. Send it to confirm a suitable pickup time with our team.

Which parts of Gaborone do you cover?

We serve Gaborone and nearby areas, including the CBD, Broadhurst, Phakalane, Tlokweng and Mogoditshane. Ask us to confirm your location.

How long will my order take?

Turnaround depends on the service and items. We'll confirm the expected timing when you arrange your pickup.

Can you clean delicate or special-care items?

Share the item and its care-label details with us first. We'll confirm whether we can help and how it should be handled.
